Before you drive

Requirements

All documents must be clear, legible and up to date. Booked by phone or WhatsApp? Complete your verification here.

  • Licence & NI number

    A clear photo of your driving licence (front and back) and your National Insurance number.

  • Proof of address

    • PDF bank statement from your online banking, dated within the last 2 months
    • A recent utility bill
    • One more, such as a letter from an official body

  • Driver questions

    Your age, how long you've held your licence, points, disqualifications, current insurance and any previous claims. Some answers need a quick call.

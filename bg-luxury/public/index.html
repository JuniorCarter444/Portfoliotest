Good to know

Requirements & FAQ

What do I need to rent?

You must be 25 or older and have a valid driver's licence, full-coverage insurance that transfers to rentals, and a major credit card in your name.

How does the deposit work?

You can reserve with a deposit and pay the balance at pickup, or pay in full online. A separate refundable security hold goes on your card at pickup and is released after the car is returned in the same condition.

Is there a mileage limit?

Each rental includes a daily mileage allowance. Extra miles are billed at the vehicle's overage rate.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel more than 72 hours before pickup for a full refund of your deposit. Later cancellations may forfeit the deposit.

Do you deliver?

Yes. Add a delivery address when you book and we'll confirm the delivery fee with you.

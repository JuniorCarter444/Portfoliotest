Before you book

Driver requirements

  • Aged 25+

    Minimum age 25. Some supercars may require drivers to be older.

  • Full licence

    Full UK or EU driving licence held for at least 3 years.

  • ID & address

    Photo ID plus proof of address dated within the last 3 months.

  • Security deposit

    Refundable deposit taken at collection. The amount depends on the vehicle.

The small print

Hire terms

Reservations & payment

Reserve online with a 25% deposit or pay in full. Any balance is due at collection.

Security deposit

A refundable security deposit is taken at collection and released once the car is returned in the same condition.

Cancellations

Cancel more than 72 hours before collection for a full refund of your reservation payment. Later cancellations may forfeit it.

Mileage & fuel

Each hire includes a daily mileage allowance. Excess miles are charged per mile. Please return the car with the same fuel level.

Fines, tolls & PCNs

The hirer is responsible for parking fines, speeding tickets, ULEZ/congestion charges and tolls during the hire, plus an admin fee.

Music video & photo shoots

Cars are available by the hour for music videos and shoots (Urus from £200/hr). Send an enquiry with your date, location and brief.
